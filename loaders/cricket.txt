Cricket is a popular bat-and-ball sport played between two teams of eleven players. The aim is to score more runs than the opposing team. One team bats to make runs by hitting the ball and running between the wickets, while the other team bowls and fields to take wickets and restrict scoring.

Cricket is played in different formats. Test matches can last up to five days and are known for patience, technique, and long-term strategy. One Day Internationals (ODIs) have 50 overs per side and balance careful planning with aggressive play. T20 matches have 20 overs per side and are fast, exciting, and focused on quick scoring.

The game rewards many skills: batting requires timing and shot selection, bowling involves speed, swing, seam, and spin, and fielding demands agility, catching, and accurate throwing. Major tournaments like the Cricket World Cup and the T20 World Cup have made the sport famous worldwide, especially in countries such as India, Australia, England, Pakistan, South Africa, New Zealand, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, and Afghanistan.
